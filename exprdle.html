Before you begin
Each expression has a minimum of 2 numbers separated by an operator, with a 1 in 4 chance to keep adding a number and an operator. This repeats until it fails. Additionally, each number has a 1 in 7 chance to become negative. Each number also has a 1 in 7 chance to gain a decimal digit, with each extra decimal digit being another 1 in 3 (also repeats until fail). Basic operators are more common, while more complicated operators are much rarer. + − × ÷ ^ are common, sin/cos/tan are rare, ∫ and d/dx are the rarest of them all. Calculus terms are evaluated exactly, and it even uses the same simplifier and evaluator as Mathillion! Pretty cool, right? There are currently badges, and each one is worth 100 / p Mag, which for now p is based on the simulation of a few thousand rolls, all calculated before you even hit the Roll button.
